### Kısa (≤160)

### Uzun
DegenKit is a non-custodial, no-code token launcher. Create a fixed-supply token on Solana, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base or Robinhood Chain from your own wallet in one signature. On Solana, mint and freeze authority are revoked and metadata is locked automatically; EVM chains support plain ERC-20/BEP-20 and custom buy/sell tax tokens with no mint function. Extra tools include Swap, Create/Remove Liquidity (Raydium), Airdrop, Multisender and a token Scanner. DegenKit never holds keys or funds.
